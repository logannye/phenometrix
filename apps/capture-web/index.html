Nonclinical research prototype

Compare two facial movement sessions, locally.

This demo is for a participant with previously established unilateral peripheral facial palsy. It reports engineering measurements and raw differences between two live sessions without interpreting health change.

Not for new or sudden facial weakness

This prototype does not diagnose facial palsy, grade severity, screen for stroke, or recommend care. New or sudden symptoms require ordinary clinical or emergency evaluation outside this demo.

Before you begin

  • Use a quiet, well-lit space and face the camera.
  • Use headphones if another person’s voice may be audible.
  • Raw media and results are not uploaded or saved.
  • This prototype does not verify identity, affected side, or provide clinical interpretation.
